The Open Emotion Wheel about page credits:

- Willcox, G. (1982). The Feeling Wheel: A tool for expanding awareness of
  emotions and increasing spontaneity and intimacy. Transactional Analysis
  Journal, 12(4), 274-276. https://doi.org/10.1177/036215378201200411

Changes made in Open Feelings:

- Converted the wheel terms into Swift data structures for an iOS app.
- Rendered the content as native SwiftUI segments and controls.
- Added local logging, iCloud sync, export, reminders, app lock, and optional
  Apple Health integration around the taxonomy.
