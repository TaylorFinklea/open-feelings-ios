# Clinical Reference Text

Open Feelings includes short emotion descriptions to help users name what they
are feeling. These descriptions are original educational summaries written for
the app. They are not copied clinical dictionary entries, diagnostic criteria,
or treatment guidance.

The summaries are clinically informed by these references:

- APA Dictionary of Psychology: https://www.apa.org/pubs/books/4311007.html
- NIH Toolbox Emotion Domain scoring and interpretation guide: https://resources.nihtoolbox.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Toolbox_Scoring_and_Interpretation_Guide_for_iPad_v1.7-5.25.21.pdf
- NIMH anxiety disorders topic page: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/anxiety-disorders
- NIMH RDoC acute threat/fear construct: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/constructs/acute-threat-fear
- NIH Clinical Center loss, grief, and bereavement material: https://www.cc.nih.gov/bereavement
- Open Emotion Wheel v1.1 taxonomy: https://openemotionwheel.com/

The definitions are intended to support reflection during check-ins. They should
not be presented as professional evaluation, diagnosis, or medical advice.
